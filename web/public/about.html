About Housedge

Housedge shows brands what AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude tell their customers about them, and helps change the answer.

What Housedge does

Housedge is an AI visibility service, sometimes called generative engine optimisation (GEO). Every week it asks AI assistants the questions a brand’s customers ask, in each of the brand’s markets and languages, and keeps every answer. It shows which brands the assistants recommend, in what order and in what tone, with the exact sentence as proof and the websites each answer relied on.

It then gives a short list of what to change so the brand is recommended: the comparison and review sites to be on, the facts to publish, the pages to fix. Every change is dated, and Housedge measures how often the brand was named in the 28 days before and after it. Clients can use Housedge on their own, or have the Housedge team do the work with them.

Who it is for

Marketing and brand teams at companies that people ask AI about:

  • Fintech and money apps: “is it safe?” and “which app pays faster?” are now asked to AI first.
  • Consumer apps and brands: comparison questions in every market they sell in.
  • Regulated industries such as iGaming, health and finance, where every action must be honest and compliant.
  • Agencies that manage brands for clients and need proof and monthly reports.

Products and services

  • AI visibility tracking: weekly answers from AI assistants to your customers’ questions, per assistant and per market
  • Share of answers: how often you are named against each competitor, with the change week by week
  • Proof for every finding: the exact quote from the answer and the sources the assistant used
  • Action list: what to change to be recommended, in order of impact
  • Work log: every action dated and measured 28 days before and after
  • Website check and brand profile: the facts AI should know about you, with ready-to-paste page content
  • Outreach: the sites AI cites that do not list you yet, with draft emails
  • Reports for teams and agencies, and a data API
  • Done-with-you service: the Housedge team carries out the listings, pages, facts and outreach

Markets

Housedge works for brands in any country. Questions are asked in each client’s own markets and languages, for example Finland, the United Kingdom, the United States, Pakistan and Nigeria, because AI assistants recommend different brands in different countries.

Trust and security

  • Data kept in the European Union (Dublin, Ireland).
  • Each client only ever sees its own data, enforced by the database itself.
  • No cookies, no tracking, and client data is never used to train AI.
  • Honest methods only: no fake or paid reviews, no anonymous posts, no promised rankings.
