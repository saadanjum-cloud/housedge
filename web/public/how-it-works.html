How it works

Five steps, every week. One number that goes up.

The number is your share of answers: how often AI assistants name your brand when your customers ask about your category.

1. Ask

We put your customers’ questions to the AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude), in each of your markets and languages, every week. Real answers, with the web searches behind them.

2. Read

Every answer is read the same way: which brands it names, in what order, in what tone, and which websites it relied on. Every finding keeps its quote; if the quote is not in the answer, the finding is thrown out.

3. Show

You see your share of answers against your competitors, per assistant and per market, and how it changes week by week.

4. Fix

A short list of what moves the answer for you: the comparison and review sites to be on, the facts to publish, the pages to fix. Most of it we can do with you.

5. Measure

Every change is dated. We compare how often you were named in the 28 days before and after, so you learn what actually works.

Set-up

Sign up, enter your website, and Housedge drafts your set-up from it: your brand, your competitors, your markets and the questions your customers ask. You check every step before the first answers come in.
