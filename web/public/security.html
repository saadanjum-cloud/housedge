Security

Your data, kept safe and kept yours.

Where data is kept

All client data is stored in the European Union, in Dublin, Ireland, with our database provider Supabase. The website is served by Vercel.

Separation between clients

Every client only ever sees its own data. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not only by the app, and it is tested automatically.

Sign-in

  • Passwords of at least 10 characters, with upper case, lower case and a number.
  • Signing out ends the session on every device; idle sessions end automatically.
  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS, with strict transport security).

Privacy

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Client data is never used to train AI.
  • To read AI answers, Housedge sends your tracked questions to the AI providers that run the assistants.
