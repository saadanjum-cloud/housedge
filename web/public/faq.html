FAQ

Questions people ask us.

What is Housedge?

Housedge is an AI visibility service. It shows brands what AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude tell their customers about them, who gets recommended instead and why, and what to change.

Which AI assistants does Housedge track?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. Each one reads a different part of the web, so a brand can be the top pick in one and missing in another.

Is this just SEO?

No. Search ranks pages; assistants write one answer and name a few brands. Being named depends on what the web says about you across many sites, how clearly your facts are published, and whether the assistant can verify you. Different levers, different measurement.

Can Housedge change AI answers directly?

Nobody can. We change what AI reads: the comparison pages you are on, the facts on your site, the reviews and communities where you are discussed. Then we measure whether the answers moved.

How often are the answers checked?

Every week, for every question, in every market you track. You can also run a check at any time.

Which countries and languages?

Any. Questions are asked in each client’s own markets and languages, because assistants recommend different brands in different countries.

What do I get in the free snapshot?

Your brand against your competitors on the questions your customers ask, across the AI assistants, in your markets, with the first actions worth taking. Set-up takes a few minutes: Housedge reads your website and drafts it for you.

Where is my data kept?

In the European Union (Dublin, Ireland). Every client only ever sees its own data, enforced by the database itself, and we never use client data to train AI.

Is it honest?

Yes. No paid or fake reviews, no anonymous posts, no promises of rankings. Every number comes with its evidence, and every action we suggest is one you could show to a regulator.

Who is Housedge for?

Marketing and brand teams at fintech and money apps, consumer apps and brands, regulated industries such as iGaming, health and finance, and agencies.
